# PolyPalette Privacy Notice

Version 1.1 — effective 28 September 2026

This notice covers the PolyPalette application and its 3ds Max and Blender plug-ins.

PolyPalette is operated by Jevz, from Alberta, Canada. For anything to do with this notice or your information, reach us on the PolyPalette Discord server or by email to zannajev@gmail.com.

---

## 1. Information we collect

We collect information for two purposes: to confirm your licence is valid and being used within its terms, and to gather usage metrics that help us improve the product and make recommendations about it.

**Licence information.** PolyPalette contacts our licensing service when you activate a licence or start a trial, and each time you launch it. These requests carry your licence key (if you have one), a device identifier and your IP address, and for a trial we also record when it started.

**Order information.** Gumroad, who handle your purchase, pass us your order details, including the name on the order and the licence tier you bought.

**Usage information.** We record technical and usage data linked to your device identifier. This may include your computer name, the versions of PolyPalette and your host application, an approximate location derived from your IP address, general activity information, and limited network information. We use it to improve PolyPalette and to recommend the licence tier that suits how you use it.

**Messages and files you send us.** If you contact us, we use your message and contact details to answer you, and a file you send privately to report a problem is used only to reproduce or fix that problem and is not shared without your permission. Feedback and suggestions may be used and shared as the licence agreement describes, but your contact details are handled only as this notice says.

Your licence agreement requires the licence information above; without it PolyPalette cannot confirm your licence and will not run.

---

## 2. Use of AI

Any use of AI with PolyPalette, whether inside PolyPalette or through an AI application you connect to it, is optional and uses an AI service **you** choose, with your own account. Anything sent that way goes straight from your machine to that service under its own terms and privacy policy. **None of it reaches us**, and we do not receive, store or process it.

---

## 3. Who else handles your information

**Cloudflare, Inc.** hosts our licensing and usage service and processes this information on our behalf.

**Gumroad, Inc.** is the merchant of record for your purchase and handles payment, orders and refunds. Their own privacy policy governs your purchase.

We do not sell your information and we do not share it with anyone beyond the parties above.

---

## 4. How long we keep it

Licence, device and order information is kept while the licence is active, and afterwards only as long as needed to handle refunds, disputes and legal or accounting obligations, then deleted. Detailed per-device activity is kept for three months, after which only anonymous totals remain. Trial records are deleted automatically after one year. Messages and files you send us are kept only as long as needed to deal with them.

---

## 5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, give you a copy in a portable form, correct anything inaccurate, delete it where we are not obliged to keep it, restrict how we use it, or object to any use we base on our legitimate interests, including usage metrics.

Contact us as shown at the top of this notice and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta, or your own national data protection authority.

Where the UK or EU GDPR applies, our lawful basis is performance of our contract with you for licence and order information, our legitimate interest in improving the product and recommending the right version of it for usage information, and our legitimate interest in answering you and fixing problems you report for messages and files you send us, and in improving the product for feedback you send us.

Your information may be processed outside your own country, including in Canada and the United States. Where the law requires it, we protect it with an adequacy decision or standard data protection clauses, and you can ask us for a copy of the safeguard we rely on.

---

## 6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, including encrypting it in transit and restricting who can access it.

---

## 7. Changes

We may update this notice; the current version ships with the latest release of PolyPalette, numbered and dated at the top, and material changes are announced in the release notes and on the PolyPalette Discord server.
